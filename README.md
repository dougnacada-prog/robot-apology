A robot's two-line confession: one sentence owning a specific mistake, one naming the lesson and the fix. The entry:

> I am sorry I reminded you of your dentist appointment one day late and one tooth too late.
> From now on I remind you the day before, and then again the morning of.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the apology to `ENTRY_OUTPUT` or `/output/entry.txt` — exactly two lines, 162 characters, no trailing newline.
